Delete your account

H-sib Workshop. Last updated 28 October 2026.

You can delete everything the Workshop keeps about you, and your sign in record, yourself.

In the app

Open H-sib Workshop, then Settings, then Account, then tap Delete my cloud data and tap it again to confirm. This deletes:

  • your designs that were copied to your account,
  • your profile of counters,
  • your record of accepting the Terms of use,
  • your handle and the apps you published in the Atelier,
  • the ratings you gave to other apps (your words and your handle; a star may remain counted in an average),
  • your sign in record with the Workshop.

You are signed out. Your designs and apps on the phone stay on the phone.

If you cannot open the app

Open an issue at the address below and say which handle you use (do not write your email in it). We will tell you how to prove the account is yours, and then delete the same data.

What stays

Anything on your phone stays on your phone until you remove it or uninstall the Workshop. Google keeps its own record of your Google account; that is yours to manage in your Google account settings.
