1. What the Workshop is

H-sib Workshop is an app for Android phones that lets you design and make your own small Android apps ("your apps") from ready made starting points and a design editor. The Workshop builds your app as a file on your phone and signs it with a key that is kept on your phone, locked by the phone's secure key store. The Workshop does not install your apps for you and does not publish them anywhere: you decide whether to save, share or install the file.
