Your data lives on your phone

The Workshop and the apps you make keep their data on the phone where they are installed. Data is lost if the app is uninstalled, if its data is cleared, or if the phone is lost, damaged or reset. Keep your signing keys safe

If the key of an app is lost and there is no key backup, no update of that app can be installed over the old one. Save a key backup (Settings and the details of each app) and keep the file and the passphrase in a safe place. The Workshop does not store your passphrase and cannot recover it.
